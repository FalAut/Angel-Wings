# Angel Wings

Equip a pair of angel wings and fly freely in survival, inspired by the Angel Ring from Extra Utilities.

## About

Angel Wings adds a simple and direct curio item: **Angel Wings**.

Once equipped in a Curios slot, the wings grant you creative-style flight immediately. There is no fuel, no energy system, no machines, and no extra unlock requirements. Equip the wings to fly, remove them to lose the flight ability.

When worn, the wings are rendered on your player model, so they are visible on your back instead of being just another invisible utility item.

## Features

- Adds an equippable Angel Wings curio
- Grants creative-style flight while equipped
- No fuel, no energy, and no extra activation conditions
- Renders wings on the player's back
- Great for survival building, exploration, and modpacks that want simple flight

## How To Use

1. Install this mod together with Curios.
2. Get the Angel Wings item in-game.
3. Open your Curios inventory and equip the wings.
4. Double-tap jump, just like in creative mode, to start flying.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge
- Curios API

## Who Is This For?

Angel Wings is for players who want a lightweight flight option without a complicated progression system. If you enjoy building, exploring, or simply want to avoid constant scaffolding and long travel in survival, this mod lets you equip the wings and start flying right away.
